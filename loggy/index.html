What does Loggy3 record?

Loggy3 records your screen, computer inputs, and metadata. Your recordings and personally identifiable information are not sold to advertisers.

How much do I earn per hour?

Your hourly rate depends on your tier: Basic, Apprentice, Advanced, or Pro. New contributors start on Basic. Your tier depends on the kind of work you record. Skilled work like CAD, Excel, and programming earns higher tiers. Casual browsing and entertainment may earn lower tiers. Tiers are reviewed every pay cycle. Your tier is shown in the sidebar and your rate next to the Start button, and the app lets you know whenever your rate changes. Each recording is paid at the rate in effect when it started, for up to 10 hours per UTC day.

When will I receive my first payment?

Payments, including your first payment, are processed on the 1st and 15th of each month. Recordings count toward the pay period in which they finish uploading (UTC): the 1st–14th, or the 15th to the end of the month. Expect 1–3 days for processing. To confirm the exact date or check your payment status, email support@loggy3.com.

When is a referral bonus paid?

Where do I enter my payment details?

You don’t need an existing Dots account or to sign up in advance. When your first payout is processed on the 1st or 15th, you’ll receive an email from Dots with a link to set up your payout account and enter your payment details. Expect 1–3 days for processing.

What withdrawal methods do you support?

We support GCash, PayMaya, GrabPay, M-Pesa, UPI, PayPal, AirTM, Payoneer, and more through our payment partner Dots. Availability varies by country. Learn more here.

Is there a minimum withdrawal or payout fee?

There is no minimum withdrawal amount and no payout fee.

Does it record my microphone or webcam?

No. Loggy3 does not record your microphone or webcam.

What if I accidentally record something private?

Start and stop recording whenever you'd like. After you stop, there's a delay before your recording uploads.

To delete a recording before it uploads, hover over the recording you wish to delete and a red trash can will appear

Can I stop recording whenever I want?

Yes. You can stop recording whenever you like. You’re paid for active recording time, and there’s no minimum number of hours you need to record each day.

What counts as active recording time?

Active time is time spent using your computer with inputs such as typing and clicking. Loggy3 automatically pauses after a period of inactivity. Most everyday computer use counts as active time. You can track your active time and see when recording is paused in the app.

Do not use bots, auto-clickers, mouse jigglers, or similar tools to fake activity or keep recording while you’re away. Doing so will result in a ban from Loggy3.

Do I need to complete specific tasks?

No. Use your computer like you normally do. Play games, do homework, browse or shop online, or do anything else you already enjoy. There are no assigned tasks to complete.

Will Loggy3 slow down my computer?

Loggy3 is designed to run as a small background process. Most computers experience no noticeable slowdown during normal use.

Who can join, and which devices are supported?

You can join if you’re over 18 and live in a supported country. Currently, Loggy3 is available in the Philippines, Kenya, India, and Pakistan. You can use it on a computer running Windows 10, Windows 11, or macOS.
